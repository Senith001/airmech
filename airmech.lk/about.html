About us

Air-conditioning engineers who stay with you after the sale

Air Mech Engineering Works designs, supplies, installs and maintains air conditioning for homes and businesses in Sri Lanka, and now brings the Macma range to the country.

Who we are

An engineering company first

We are air-conditioning engineers first, and that shapes how we work. Every recommendation starts with the room: its size, how much sun it gets, how it’s used and what the wiring can take. We size the system to fit, install it to the manufacturer’s standard and keep it running with regular servicing.

Our customers range from families cooling a single bedroom to shops and offices running several units all day. Whatever the size of the job, the same engineers who quote it are the ones who install it and come back to service it.

How we work

What you can expect from us

  • Right-sized, not oversold

    We recommend the capacity your room needs, not the most expensive unit on the list. A correctly sized system cools better and costs less to run.

  • Installed properly

    Correct pipe runs, a vacuumed and pressure-tested system, and a clean finish. Good installation decides how well an air conditioner performs for its whole life.

  • There after the sale

    Scheduled servicing, quick responses when something isn’t right, and warranty support handled by the team you already know.

Our partnership

Why we chose Macma

When we looked for a range to put our name behind, we wanted three things: inverter efficiency as standard, hardware that stands up to tropical heat and coastal air, and pricing that makes sense for Sri Lankan homes and businesses.

Macma ticks all three. And because we supply the range directly, the team that sells your unit is the same team that installs it, services it and handles its warranty.
