What makes a Macma

Four things every Macma is built to do

Every model in the range shares the same engineering priorities. Here’s what they mean for you day to day.

01

100%

of Macma models use a full DC inverter

Efficient by design

A fixed-speed air conditioner switches its compressor fully on and off all day. Macma inverters adjust their speed continuously instead, holding the temperature steady and using noticeably less electricity, especially when the unit runs for hours at a time.

    02

    52 °C

    maximum outdoor temperature for rated cooling

    Built for the tropics

    Outdoor units in Sri Lanka face direct sun, monsoon humidity, salt air near the coast and an electricity supply that isn’t always steady. Macma hardware is specified for those conditions from the start.

      03

      20 dB(A)

      sleep-mode noise on Macma Prime

      Comfort you barely notice

      A good air conditioner fades into the background. Macma indoor units are tuned to run quietly, cool quickly when you walk into a hot room, and stay gentle through the night.

        04

        Wi-Fi

        phone control built into Macma Prime

        Cleaner air, smarter control

        Humidity and dust are hard on indoor air. Self-cleaning coils, layered filters and a dedicated dry mode keep it fresher, and Prime adds Wi-Fi so you can cool the room before you arrive.
