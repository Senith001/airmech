FAQ

Common questions

Can’t find your answer? Ask us directly.

How often should an air conditioner be serviced?

With Sri Lanka’s dust and humidity, we recommend a basic service every three to four months for homes. Shops and offices that run their units all day may need it more often. Clean filters and coils keep the unit efficient and help it last longer.

What size air conditioner do I need?

It depends on floor area, sun exposure, the roof above and how many people use the room. Our sizing guide gives a quick estimate, and we confirm it on site before you buy.

Is an inverter air conditioner worth it?

If the unit runs for more than a few hours a day, almost always. An inverter avoids the constant stop-start of a fixed-speed unit, so it typically uses noticeably less electricity and holds a steadier temperature.

Do you service brands other than Macma?

Yes. We service and repair most major air-conditioner brands as well as Macma.

Can I get a maintenance contract for my business?

Yes. Tell us how many units you have and where they are, and we’ll send a proposal with a visit schedule.
